Partners are organizations that build on, integrate with, or extend the Orivon
platform. Partnership is about mutual value: partners get a better platform;
the ecosystem gets more capabilities.

## Partner types

| Type | Description | Typical partners |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Technology partners** | Integrate Orivon into their stack or build on Orivon APIs | Cloud providers, hardware vendors, framework maintainers |
| **Ecosystem partners** | Build applications, modules, or services for Orivon users | Wallet builders, identity providers, marketplace operators |
| **Integration partners** | Connect Orivon to external protocols, chains, or standards | Bridge operators, oracle networks, standards bodies |
| **Distribution partners** | Help users discover and adopt Orivon | Browser directories, app stores, enterprise resellers |
| **Research partners** | Collaborate on research, standards, and open problems | Universities, research institutes, standards orgs |

## Become a partner

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify fit">
    Review the partner types and see where your organization aligns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit interest">
    Fill out the partner interest form (To Be Announced) with your use case
    and desired partnership type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discuss">
    The Partnerships team (Community WG + TSC liaison) reviews and schedules
    a call to align on scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree">
    Sign a partner agreement (non-exclusive, no governance rights, public
    listing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Technical onboarding, directory listing, and joint announcement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Partnership principles

<CheckList
  items={[
    'Non-exclusive: partners are free to work with other platforms.',
    'No governance rights: partnership does not confer decision-making authority.',
    'Public by default: partnerships are announced and listed publicly.',
    'Mutual value: both sides must benefit measurably.',
    'Standards-first: integrations follow published standards, not private APIs.',
  ]}
/>

## Partner responsibilities

- Maintain the integration to current Orivon standards.
- Communicate breaking changes in advance.
- Participate in security disclosure coordination.
- Keep directory listing accurate.
