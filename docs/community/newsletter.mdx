The Orivon newsletter is a monthly curated digest delivered to your inbox. It
is the easiest way to stay current without watching every channel.

## What's in each issue

| Section | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Release notes** | Stable, beta, and nightly highlights. |
| **Community spotlight** | Contributor journey, project launch, or meetup report. |
| **Ecosystem updates** | New projects, grants awarded, hackathon results. |
| **Governance** | TSC decisions, working group updates, proposals to review. |
| **Events** | Upcoming meetups, workshops, hackathons, office hours. |
| **Research** | New papers, architecture posts, research roadmap progress. |
| **Jobs & opportunities** | Grants, bounties, hiring, mentorship, speaking slots. |

## Subscribe

**Email:** newsletter@orivon.dev (or use the form at newsletter.orivon.dev)

- **Frequency:** Monthly (first Tuesday of each month).
- **Format:** HTML + plain text.
- **Archive:** All past issues at [newsletter.orivon.dev/archive](https://newsletter.orivon.dev/archive).
- **Unsubscribe:** One-click in every issue.

<Callout type="info" title="No spam, ever">
  We send one email per month. No marketing, no third-party promotions, no
  tracking pixels beyond open rates. Your email is not shared.
</Callout>

## Contributing to the newsletter

The newsletter is curated by the [Community Working Group](/docs/community/working-groups/community).
To suggest content:

- **Community spotlight:** Nominate via the showcase form.
- **Ecosystem updates:** Post in the forum #ecosystem channel.
- **Events:** Add to the community calendar.
- **Jobs/opportunities:** Email community@orivon.dev.

## Newsletter vs. other channels

| Channel | Frequency | Depth | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Newsletter** | Monthly | Curated summary | Staying current efficiently |
| **Blog** | Bi-weekly | Deep dives | Understanding *why* |
| **Twitter/X** | Daily | Real-time | Breaking announcements |
| **Discord** | Continuous | Conversational | Quick questions, collaboration |
| **Forum** | Daily | Threaded | Design, governance, RFCs |
| **GitHub** | Daily | Technical | Code, issues, releases |
