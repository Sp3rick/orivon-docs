Sponsors provide the financial foundation that keeps Orivon independent,
sustainable, and community-governed. Sponsorship does not buy control — it
supports the mission.

## Sponsorship tiers

| Tier | Annual commitment | Benefits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Community Friend** | $1k–$10k | Logo on sponsors page, newsletter mention, community Discord role. |
| **Supporter** | $10k–$50k | Above + swag kit, event tickets, quarterly impact report. |
| **Partner** | $50k–$200k | Above + dedicated Slack channel, roadmap input session, speaking slot at summit. |
| **Premier Partner** | $200k+ | Above + annual strategy meeting, early access to releases, co-branded content. |

<Callout type="info" title="Sponsorship ≠ governance">
  Sponsors do not get votes, veto power, or special access to decision-making.
  Governance is community-owned. See [Governance](/docs/community/governance).
</Callout>

## What sponsorship funds

- Infrastructure: CI/CD, hosting, package registry, documentation site.
- Programs: grants, hackathons, meetups, travel fund, mentorship.
- Security: audits, bug bounties, disclosure coordination.
- Community: events, swag, translation, accessibility.
- Operations: legal, accounting, project management.

## Become a sponsor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review tiers">
    Choose the tier that fits your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact">
    Email sponsors@orivon.dev with your interest and tier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree">
    Sign the sponsorship agreement (standard terms, no governance rights).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    Your logo goes live; benefits begin immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sponsorship agreement highlights

- **No governance rights.** Sponsorship is support, not control.
- **Public by default.** Sponsor name, tier, and start date are published.
- **Annual renewal.** Sponsorships renew annually; either party may opt out.
- **Funds are unrestricted.** The project allocates funds per the community
  roadmap and budget.
