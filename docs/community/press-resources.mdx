## Embargo policy

- Orivon does not use embargoes for routine releases.
- For major announcements (governance changes, security incidents, major
  releases), a 24–48 hour embargo may be offered to registered journalists.
- To register for embargoed announcements, email press@orivon.dev with your
  publication and contact info.

<Callout type="info" title="Accuracy first">
  We prioritize giving journalists accurate, complete information over
