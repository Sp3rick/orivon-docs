The **Community working group** coordinates the health of the community:
events, the code of conduct, recognition programs, and the experience of
participating in Orivon.

## Mission

Grow a community that is welcoming, sustainable, and effective — where people
want to stay and contribute long-term.

## Typical work

- Organizing and supporting [Meetups](/docs/community/meetups) and
  [Hackathons](/docs/community/hackathons).
- Reviewing and updating the [Code of Conduct](/docs/contributors/code-of-conduct).
- Running the [Contribution Recognition](/docs/community/contribution-recognition)
  program.
- Curating the [Community Showcase](/docs/community/community-showcase).
