The **Research working group** coordinates Orivon's long-form research program:
whitepapers, architecture papers, design documents, and exploratory research
areas.

## Mission

Publish rigorous, citable research that advances the state of the web — and
make it open for the community to review, challenge, and extend.

## Scope

<CheckList
  items={[
    'Whitepapers for major subsystems and standards.',
    'Design documents recording architectural decisions.',
    'Architecture papers analyzing subsystem trade-offs.',
    'Active research areas: security, networking, storage, identity, and more.',
    'Future technology exploration: decentralized computing, AI, and the future internet.',
  ]}
/>

## Typical work

- Authoring and reviewing papers for the [Research Library](/docs/research/research-library).
- Maintaining the [Research Roadmap](/docs/research/research-roadmap).
- Running the open review process for draft and experimental papers.
- Coordinating with the Standards and Technical Steering Committee.
