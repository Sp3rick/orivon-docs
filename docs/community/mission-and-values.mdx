Everything Orivon builds and every decision the community makes is grounded in
a shared mission and a small set of values. They are the standard against which
proposals, features, and partnerships are measured.

## Mission

**To build a browser and platform that returns control of the web to its
users.**

Orivon exists because the web's most important decisions — about privacy,
identity, data, and monetization — are increasingly made by a handful of
platforms rather than by the people who use them. Our mission is to restore
user agency at every layer: how users browse, how they prove identity, how
they own data, and how they are compensated for the value they create.

## Vision

A web where:

- **Users** control their data, identity, and attention.
- **Developers** build without asking permission from a platform.
- **Participants** are rewarded for the value they contribute.
- **Innovation** happens in the open, under community governance.

## Core values

| Value | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| **Transparency** | Processes, decisions, budgets, and roadmaps are public. |
| **Openness** | Anyone can participate, propose, review, and lead. |
| **User agency** | Users, not platforms, make the decisions that affect them. |
| **Privacy by design** | Privacy is a default, not a feature flag. |
| **Security first** | Capability-based permissions and defense in depth. |
| **Community governance** | Power is distributed, not concentrated. |
| **Quality** | Standards and rigorous review protect the ecosystem. |
| **Respect** | Every interaction follows the [Code of Conduct](/docs/contributors/code-of-conduct). |

## Long-term goals

| Horizon | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| Short term | Ship the core platform, publish stable standards, grow the contributor base. |
| Medium term | Launch the Web3 Score, grants program, and ecosystem directory. |
| Long term | Establish an independent foundation and a self-sustaining ecosystem. |

The milestones against these goals are tracked on the [Project Roadmap](/docs/community/project-roadmap).

## Measuring success

The community measures success not by how many users a platform captures, but
by:

- **User agency** — how much control users have over their data and identity.
- **Openness** — how many people contribute, propose, and lead.
- **Ecosystem health** — the number and quality of ecosystem projects.
- **Trust** — the integrity of governance and the absence of rent-seeking.

<Callout type="note" title="Values are commitments">
  When a proposal conflicts with a core value, the value wins. That is a
  deliberate choice designed to keep the project aligned with its mission even
  under pressure.
</Callout>
