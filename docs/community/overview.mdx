The Community, Governance & Ecosystem section is the public home of Orivon's
community. It documents how the project is organized, how decisions are made,
how anyone can participate, and how the ecosystem grows — through programs,
partnerships, and shared values.

This section is for contributors, maintainers, organizations, partners,
researchers, sponsors, and community members who want to be part of Orivon's
growth.

## Purpose of the community

Orivon is built on a simple premise: a browser that returns control of the web
to its users can only be built by the people it serves. The community exists
to make that possible — bringing together engineers, researchers, designers,
organizations, and users who share the mission.

A healthy community is itself a platform feature: it is the source of ideas,
the early warning system for problems, and the long-term steward of the
project after any single contributor moves on.

## How the ecosystem is organized

The ecosystem is organized into the platform (browser, runtime, core, module
system), the standards that keep everything interoperable, and the community
that develops, governs, and grows both.

## Governance overview

Orivon is governed by the community through a layered structure designed for
transparency and scalability:

| Body | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| [Maintainers](/docs/community/maintainers) | Own individual repositories and areas. |
| [Working Groups](/docs/community/working-groups) | Coordinate cross-cutting topics. |
| [Technical Steering Committee](/docs/community/technical-steering-committee) | Technical direction and standards. |
| [Decision Making](/docs/community/decision-making) | The proposal, review, voting, and appeals process. |

The full model, including voting and conflict resolution, is documented on the
[Governance](/docs/community/governance) page.

## Community values

Every part of this section is grounded in a shared set of values:

- **Transparency** — decisions, processes, and budgets are public.
- **Openness** — anyone can participate, propose, and lead.
- **Innovation** — new ideas are welcomed and tested rigorously.
- **Respect** — all interaction follows the [Code of Conduct](/docs/contributors/code-of-conduct).
- **User agency** — the community exists to serve users, not platforms.
