Hackathons are focused, time-boxed build events where the community creates
real projects on Orivon. They are open to all skill levels and run quarterly
with rotating themes.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register">
    Registration opens 8 weeks before. Individuals and teams welcome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Form or join a team">
    Team matching is available during the pre-hackathon week. Solo
    participants are welcome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build">
    Two weeks to build. Mentors available in Discord/forum during office
    hours. Workshops run in the first week.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Submit a repo, demo video, and short write-up by the deadline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Judging">
    Judges (maintainers, ecosystem partners, community leads) evaluate on
    criteria below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Showcase">
    Winners and notable projects featured in the [Community Showcase](/docs/community/community-showcase)
    and considered for [Grants Program](/docs/community/grants-program) fast-track.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tracks

Each hackathon has multiple tracks so different skills can shine:

| Track | Focus | Example submissions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Core Modules** | Runtime, browser, or core modules | New capability, performance optimization |
| **Applications** | End-user apps built on Orivon | Browser extension, wallet, social app |
| **Developer Tools** | CLI, debugging, testing, scaffolding | Linter, profiler, template generator |
| **Research** | Reproductions, extensions, proofs | Benchmark, formal verification, attack |
| **Community** | Docs, tutorials, translations, tooling | Guide, video series, localization |

## Judging criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| **Technical merit** — correctness, performance, architecture | 30% |
| **Ecosystem fit** — uses Orivon APIs, standards, conventions | 25% |
| **Completeness** — working demo, tests, documentation | 20% |
| **Creativity** — novel use of platform capabilities | 15% |
| **Presentation** — clear demo, write-up, reproducibility | 10% |

## Prizes

Prize pools are sponsored by the [Grants Program](/docs/community/grants-program)
and [Sponsors](/docs/community/sponsors). Typical structure:

| Place | Prize (typical) |
| --- | --- |
| 1st (per track) | $5,000 + showcase feature + grant fast-track |
| 2nd (per track) | $2,500 + showcase feature |
| 3rd (per track) | $1,000 + showcase feature |
| Community Choice | $1,000 (voted by participants) |
| Best Newcomer | $1,000 (first-time Orivon builders) |

<Note title="Prizes vary by hackathon">
  Exact prize amounts and categories are announced with each hackathon.
  All prizes are paid in USD or equivalent stablecoin.
</Note>

## Participating

- **Skill level:** All levels. Workshops and mentors support beginners.
- **Time commitment:** Flexible. Even a few evenings can produce a submission.
- **Cost:** Free to enter.
- **IP:** You retain ownership of your code. We ask for an open-source license
  (Apache 2.0 or compatible) for the submission.

## Organizing a satellite event

Communities can host local hackathon satellite events (watch parties, co-working
sessions, local judging). Contact the [Community Working Group](/docs/community/working-groups/community)
for support.

<Callout type="success" title="Build something real">
  Hackathons are not about prototypes — they are about shipping modules,
  tools, and apps that the ecosystem can use. Come build something that lasts.
</Callout>
