Case studies are deep dives into real ecosystem projects: the problem they
solved, how they used Orivon, the architecture they chose, the results they
achieved, and the lessons they learned.

## Purpose

- **Show what is possible** — concrete examples of Orivon in production.
- **Share knowledge** — architectures and patterns that worked (and didn't).
- **Inspire builders** — see how others approached similar problems.
- **Build credibility** — real usage is the best validation.

## Case study format

Each case study follows a consistent structure:

| Section | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Project** | Name, category, team, links. |
| **Problem** | What user or business problem was being solved? |
| **Why Orivon** | What made Orivon the right choice? |
| **Architecture** | High-level diagram, key modules, data flows. |
| **Implementation** | Technical details: modules used, custom capabilities, integration points. |
| **Results** | Metrics: performance, adoption, cost savings, user feedback. |
| **Lessons learned** | What worked, what didn't, what they'd do differently. |
| **Advice for others** | Practical tips for teams building similar things. |

## Submitting a case study

If you have a project in production on Orivon and want to share the story:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check readiness">
    Project should be in production ≥3 months with measurable results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft">
    Use the case study template (To Be Announced) following the format above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Submit for review by the Ecosystem Working Group and Documentation WG.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Approved case studies are published in this section and featured in the
    newsletter and showcase.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Case study vs. success story

| Aspect | Case Study | Success Story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Depth** | Technical deep dive | High-level narrative |
| **Audience** | Builders, architects | Decision makers, community |
| **Focus** | Architecture, trade-offs, metrics | Impact, journey, outcomes |
| **Length** | 1500–3000 words | 500–1000 words |

See [Success Stories](/docs/community/success-stories) for the lighter format.
