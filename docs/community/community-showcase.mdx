The Community Showcase is a monthly series that highlights the people and
projects building on Orivon. It is the community's way of saying "this is what
we are building together."

## What gets featured

<CardGrid columns={2}>
  <Card title="Ecosystem projects">
    Applications, modules, tools, and services built on Orivon.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Contributor stories">
    Profiles of contributors: their path, their work, their perspective.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Research highlights">
    Community research, reproductions, and extensions of published papers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Community initiatives">
    Meetups, hackathons, translations, mentorship, and outreach.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Nominate">
    Anyone can nominate a project, person, or initiative using the nomination
    form (linked in the forum and newsletter).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Curate">
    The [Community Working Group](/docs/community/working-groups/community)
    reviews nominations and selects the monthly feature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    The showcase is published on the blog, shared in the newsletter, and
    promoted on social channels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive">
    All showcases are archived in the [Showcase Archive](#showcase-archive)
    for permanent reference.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Nomination criteria

We look for:

- **Built on Orivon** — uses the platform, SDK, or standards.
- **Open and verifiable** — source available, claims testable.
- **Community value** — helps others learn, build, or participate.
- **Diversity** — we aim to feature different regions, backgrounds, and
  project types over time.
