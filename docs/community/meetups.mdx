## Starting a new meetup

Anyone can start a meetup. The community provides support:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the map">
    Make sure there isn't already an active meetup in your city.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Fill out the meetup organizer application (To Be Announced) with your
    city, venue ideas, and availability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get approved">
    The Community Working Group reviews and approves within 1 week.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive kit">
    You get: organizer guide, swag kit, calendar listing, promotion support,
    and a stipend budget (application required).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Schedule your first event, announce it, and start meeting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Organizer expectations

- Host at least **one event per quarter** (monthly preferred).
- Follow the [Code of Conduct](/docs/contributors/code-of-conduct) at every event.
- Report attendance and feedback after each event (lightweight form).
- Be responsive to community questions about your meetup.

<Note title="No experience required">
  You do not need to be an expert. Enthusiasm and consistency matter more.
  The organizer guide includes templates for agendas, demos, and discussions.
</Note>

## Meetup format suggestions

| Format | Description | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Demo night** | 3–5 short demos + Q&A | Showcasing local projects |
| **Workshop** | Hands-on coding session | Skill building |
| **Paper reading** | Discuss a research paper | Research community |
| **Social** | Casual drinks/coffee | Relationship building |
| **Hack session** | Collaborative coding | Building together |

Mix formats to keep it fresh.

## Organizer resources

- **Organizer guide** — templates, checklists, and tips (To Be Announced).
- **Speaker bureau** — experienced speakers for your meetup (request via CWG).
- **Swag request** — stickers, shirts, and swag for attendees.
- **Stipend program** — up to $200/event for venue/food (application required).
- **Promotion** — listed on calendar, newsletter, and social channels.

## Virtual meetups

For regions without critical mass for in-person, virtual meetups are welcome.
They follow the same organizer process and receive the same support.
