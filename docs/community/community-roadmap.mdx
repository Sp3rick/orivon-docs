The community roadmap tracks the non-technical side of the project: the
programs, events, governance milestones, and health initiatives that grow and
sustain the community. It runs in parallel with the [Project Roadmap](/docs/community/project-roadmap).

## Community roadmap philosophy

- **Programs over projects.** We invest in repeatable programs (grants, meetups,
  mentorship) rather than one-off projects.
- **Measurable health.** We track contributor retention, geographic diversity,
  and program outcomes — not just raw numbers.
- **Community-led.** The roadmap is proposed and prioritized by the
  [Community Working Group](/docs/community/working-groups/community) and
  ratified by the TSC.

## How the community roadmap is set

<Steps>
  <Step title="Community Working Group proposes">
    The CWG gathers input from meetups, forums, and surveys, then drafts the
    roadmap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Public comment">
    The draft is posted for 14 days of community comment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="TSC ratifies">
    The TSC reviews for alignment with project roadmap and resources, then
    ratifies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute and report">
    Programs are executed by the CWG and partners. Progress is reported
    quarterly in the newsletter and TSC minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Your community, your roadmap">
  The community roadmap is not set by a central team — it is proposed by the
  Community Working Group, which anyone can join. If you want to shape it,
  start by attending a CWG meeting.
</Callout>
