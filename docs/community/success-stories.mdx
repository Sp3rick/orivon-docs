## Submitting a story

Anyone in the community can submit a success story:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick your angle">
    Contributor journey, project launch, community impact, or organizational
    adoption.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write it">
    500–1000 words. Be specific: names, dates, metrics, feelings. Use the
    template (To Be Announced).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Send to the Community Working Group for light editing and scheduling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Stories are published here, in the newsletter, and on social channels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Story guidelines

- **Be honest.** Share the struggles, not just the wins.
- **Be specific.** "We reduced latency by 40%" beats "it got faster."
- **Center people.** The technology is the setting; the people are the story.
- **Credit the community.** Mention the people, channels, and programs that
  helped.

## Contributor journeys

These stories follow a common arc:

1. **Discovery** — How they found Orivon.
2. **First contribution** — The first PR, the review, the merge.
3. **Growth** — Skills built, people met, responsibilities taken.
4. **Impact** — What they have shipped or enabled.
5. **Advice** — What they would tell their past self.

## Project launches

These stories cover:

- The problem and why Orivon.
- The build process: modules, tools, challenges.
- Launch day: metrics, feedback, surprises.
- Post-launch: iteration, community, roadmap.

## Community impact

These stories highlight:

- A meetup that sparked a collaboration.
- A mentorship that changed a career.
- A hackathon project that became a startup.
- A translation that opened a region.

## Organizational adoption

These stories explore:

- The evaluation process and decision criteria.
- The integration effort and team experience.
- The business impact: cost, speed, differentiation.
- The ongoing relationship with the ecosystem.
