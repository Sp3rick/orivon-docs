Use this template when proposing a feature. Copy it into your issue and fill
in every section. See [Feature Requests](/docs/contributors/feature-requests)
for the guidance on writing a strong proposal.

```markdown
### Problem
The problem this feature solves, stated without the solution.

### Impact
Who is affected and how. Be specific about the current cost or workaround.

### Proposed solution
A concrete description of what a good solution would look like.

### Alternatives considered
Other ways to solve the problem, and why they fall short.

### Tradeoffs
Costs, risks or design consequences of the proposal.

### Success criteria
How we would know the feature worked.

### Scope
The areas this touches: runtime, core, SDK, CLI, browser, standards.
```

<Callout type="success" title="The problem is the point">
  The community evaluates the problem first and the solution second. A clear
  problem statement survives having its solution refined during discussion.
</Callout>
