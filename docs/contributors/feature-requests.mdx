Feature requests are how the roadmap is proposed. Good proposals describe
the problem clearly, scope the audience, and invite discussion of tradeoffs.
This page explains the process and how to make your proposal effective.

<Callout type="info" title="Start with the template">
  Use the [feature request template](/docs/contributors/templates/feature-request)
  when you open your proposal.
</Callout>

## The problem is the point

A feature request lives or dies on the problem statement:

<CheckList
  items={[
    'Describe the problem, not the solution.',
    'Name who is affected and how.',
    'Show the current workaround and its cost.',
    'State what success looks like, concretely.',
  ]}
/>

<Callout type="success" title="Best practice">
  "Users cannot sign without a local wallet" states a problem. "Add a hardware
  wallet plugin" states a solution. Both belong in the proposal, but the
  problem is what the community evaluates.
</Callout>

## The proposal anatomy

```markdown
### Problem
Storage providers cannot advertise capacity tiers, so users discover
providers by trial and error.

### Impact
Provider operators and users selecting storage. Affects adoption of
self-hosted storage.

### Proposed solution
Allow providers to advertise a capacity and price tier with their service
record, surfaced in the naming system.

### Alternatives
- A registry of provider ratings (heavier, slower to trust).
- Documentation only (no mechanism).

### Tradeoffs
Adds a field to service records; needs schema versioning care.

### Scope
Core services, DNS records, and the storage APIs.
```

| Stage | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Filed | Your proposal is opened |
| Discussion | Contributors refine the problem and scope |
| Decision | Maintainers accept, defer or close |
| Design | Accepted ideas go through the standards process |
| Build | A contributor implements the feature |

## What makes a proposal effective

| Effective | Less effective |
| --- | --- |
| Narrow problem, clear audience | Vague "it would be nice to have" |
| Realistic scope | "Rewrite the whole architecture" |
| Acknowledges tradeoffs | Assumes no costs |
| Suggests how to evaluate | No success criteria |

## After you file

- Engage in the discussion; refine the proposal with feedback.
- Be open to a narrower or different solution to the same problem.
- If the proposal is accepted, consider being the implementer.

<Callout type="info" title="Big features go through standards">
  Large or protocol level features are specified through the
  [standards process](/docs/standards/specification-lifecycle) before they are
  built. Your feature request is the seed of that process.
</Callout>
