Orivon is an open, global community, and our strength comes from the range of
people in it. The Code of Conduct exists to keep every space in this
community welcoming, professional and safe, for everyone, in every time zone.

<Callout type="info" title="Scope">
  This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces: repositories, issue
  trackers, discussions, chat channels, meetups, conferences and any other
  venue where the Orivon community gathers. It applies equally to newcomers
  and maintainers.
</Callout>

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio
economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Respect

Respect is the baseline of every interaction:

<CheckList
  items={[
    'Treat others with courtesy and professionalism.',
    'Assume good faith until proven otherwise.',
    'Listen before responding; everyone has context you lack.',
    'Criticize ideas, not people.',
    'Honor the time and attention of others.',
  ]}
/>

## Inclusion

An inclusive community is a stronger one:

<CheckList
  items={[
    'Welcome newcomers and their questions, however basic they seem.',
    'Use language that is accessible to non-native speakers.',
    'Avoid jargon when it excludes; explain when it is needed.',
    'Make room for quieter voices in discussions.',
    'Respect pronouns and names, and correct mistakes gracefully.',
  ]}
/>

## Professional behavior

Professional behavior is what makes long term collaboration possible:

| Do | Do not |
| --- | --- |
| Give constructive, actionable feedback | Personal attacks or insults |
| Disagree with reasons and evidence | Trolling, baiting or flaming |
| Ask clarifying questions | Dismissive or condescending replies |
| Credit others for their work | Claiming credit for others' work |
| Follow the security and privacy expectations | Sharing private information |

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are never acceptable in Orivon spaces:

<Accordion title="Harassment and discrimination">
  Any harassment based on identity, or any form of discrimination, is a
  violation. This includes unwanted advances, offensive comments, and the
  intentional use of wrong names or pronouns.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Violence and threats">
  Threats of violence, encouragement of harm, or content glorifying violence
  are immediate violations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Doxxing and privacy">
  Publishing or threatening to publish someone's private information, such as
  a physical address or private communications, without consent.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Unwanted contact">
  Sustained or unwanted communication, after being asked to stop, is
  harassment.
</Accordion>

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation, report it. Reports are taken
seriously and handled confidentially.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Report">
    Send a report to the community team at the address in the repository.
    Include what happened, when, and any evidence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confidentiality">
    Your report is confidential. The team does not share your identity with
    the subject or the wider community without your consent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Response">
    The team reviews the report and decides on action, guided by the
    enforcement section below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="In urgent situations">
  If you are in immediate danger or the behavior involves real world harm,
  contact the relevant authorities first. The community team cannot replace
  emergency services.
</Callout>

## Enforcement

The community team reviews every report and applies proportional
consequences:

| Severity | Typical response |
| --- | --- |
| First minor offense | Private warning and education |
| Repeated minor offenses | Public warning, behavior contract |
| Serious offense | Temporary or permanent ban |

Enforcement is consistent, documented and appealable. Anyone subject to a
decision can appeal in private to a senior member of the team.

<Callout type="success" title="Community expectations">
  The community is expected to help, not police. If you see a minor
  violation, a kind private note often resolves it. Escalate through the
  reporting path when behavior is harmful or repeated.
</Callout>

## Our commitment

Maintainers and moderators are held to the same standard as everyone else.
We are committed to enforcing this Code of Conduct fairly, transparently and
quickly, and to improving the community through our own example.

<Callout type="info" title="This document lives with the community">
  The Code of Conduct is a living document. Improvements are proposed through
  the normal [contribution](/docs/contributors/development-workflow) process
  and discussed openly.
</Callout>
