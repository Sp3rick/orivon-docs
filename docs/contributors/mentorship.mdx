Mentorship is how Orivon turns newcomers into contributors and contributors
into maintainers. A mentor shortens the path, answers the questions the docs
cannot, and makes the first contributions feel possible.

<Callout type="success" title="Everyone gets help">
  You do not need to know anyone or prove anything to request guidance. The
  mentorship program pairs contributors with someone who has been where you
  are.
</Callout>

## What mentors do

A mentor helps you navigate, not do the work for you:

<CheckList
  items={[
    'Point you to the right code, docs and people.',
    'Help you scope a task you chose.',
    'Explain the review process and expectations.',
    'Give feedback on your approach before the formal review.',
    'Connect you to the community and its norms.',
  ]}
/>

## How to request guidance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a task">
    Choose a [good first issue](/docs/contributors/good-first-issues) or a
    small task you want help with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for a mentor">
    Request a mentor through the community channels, or comment on the issue
    that you would like guidance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree on the mode">
    Mentors support over chat, issues and calls, depending on what works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn by doing">
    You do the work; the mentor keeps you unblocked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Asking is normal">
  Asking for help is not a sign of failure; it is the fastest way to learn.
  Every contributor, including every maintainer, started by asking.
</Callout>

## What to expect from a mentor

| Expectation | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Responsiveness | Timely answers, within reason |
| Honesty | Straight answers about quality and standards |
| Delegation | You do the work, so you learn it |
| Connection | Introductions to the right people and resources |

## What mentors expect from you

<CheckList
  items={[
    'Come with a specific question or task.',
    'Do the work and keep your mentor updated.',
    'Ask follow ups when you are stuck.',
    'Bring what you learned to your next contribution.',
    'Pay it forward by mentoring later.',
  ]}
/>

## Becoming a mentor

Mentoring deepens your own understanding:

<CheckList
  items={[
    'Answer questions in the community channels.',
    'Tag yourself as available to mentor in discussions.',
    'Review first contributions generously.',
    'Explain the why behind the review feedback.',
    'Encourage, and celebrate, your mentees progress.',
  ]}
/>

<Callout type="success" title="The best mentors teach">
  Teaching forces clarity. Contributors who mentor report that they learned
  more than their mentees. The program is a gift in both directions.
</Callout>
