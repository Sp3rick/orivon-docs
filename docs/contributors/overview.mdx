Orivon is built by the people who use it. The Contributor Guide is your entry
point: it explains who can contribute, how the process works, and where to
find help at every step.

<Callout type="success" title="The short version">
  Pick something you care about, read the relevant guide, and open your first
  contribution. You do not need permission and you do not need to be an
  expert. The community is here to help you through it.
</Callout>

## Why contribute

Contributing to Orivon is more than writing code. It is:

- **Building the open web**: every contribution moves the platform toward a
  web users own and control.
- **Learning deeply**: contributing is the best way to understand the
  architecture, from the sandbox to the standards.
- **Growing a portfolio**: your work is public, attributed and reviewed by
  people who care about quality.
- **Earning recognition**: contributors are acknowledged publicly and through
  the rewards program.

See [Why Contribute](/docs/contributors/why-contribute) for the full case.

## Who can contribute

Anyone. The guide covers contributions from:

| Who you are | How you fit in |
| --- | --- |
| **Developer** | Code, reviews, tests, performance |
| **Designer** | UI, UX, icons, illustrations, diagrams |
| **Writer** | Documentation, guides, editing |
| **Tester** | Bug hunting, QA, conformance testing |
| **Security researcher** | Responsible disclosure, audits |
| **Community member** | Support, translation, outreach, governance |

The ecosystem is bigger than the code, and every role is a real contribution.

## Community values

The work happens in an open, respectful community. The
[Code of Conduct](/docs/contributors/code-of-conduct) is the contract every
contributor lives by:

<CheckList
  items={[
    'Be respectful and constructive in every interaction.',
    'Assume good faith: most disagreements are about tradeoffs, not people.',
    'Give actionable feedback and receive it openly.',
    'Credit others for their work, always.',
    'Follow the security and privacy expectations without exception.',
  ]}
/>

## Quick start

The fastest path to a first contribution:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up">
    Install the development tools and clone the repository. See
    [Getting Started](/docs/contributors/getting-started).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find an issue">
    Start with a [good first issue](/docs/contributors/good-first-issues).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the guides">
    Follow the [development workflow](/docs/contributors/development-workflow)
    and the [coding standards](/docs/contributors/coding-standards).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a PR">
    Submit your change with the [pull request checklist](/docs/contributors/pull-requests).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Iterate">
    Engage with the review and land your contribution.
  </Step>
</Steps>
