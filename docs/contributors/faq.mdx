Short answers to the questions contributors ask most. If your question is
not here, the [troubleshooting](/docs/contributors/troubleshooting) page
covers problems, and the [community guidelines](/docs/contributors/community-guidelines)
explain where to ask.

## Getting started

<Accordion title="Do I need to be an expert to contribute?">
  No. You need to care about a problem and be willing to learn. The
  [good first issues](/docs/contributors/good-first-issues) are sized for
  newcomers and [mentorship](/docs/contributors/mentorship) supports you.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long until my first contribution merges?">
  A small, well prepared change can merge within days. Larger changes take
  longer, mostly through review and iteration. Speed comes from following the
  [workflow](/docs/contributors/development-workflow).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if I do not know where to start?">
  Ask. Post in the community discussions or pick a
  [good first issue](/docs/contributors/good-first-issues). Stating "I want
  to help but do not know how" is a completely normal start.
</Accordion>

## The workflow

<Accordion title="Can I work directly on the repository?">
  No. Work on a fork and open a pull request. See
  [Development Workflow](/docs/contributors/development-workflow).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My pull request failed CI. What do I do?">
  Read the failing check and reproduce it locally. Fix the failure and push.
  If you cannot figure it out, ask in the PR or the community.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I pick a branch name?">
  Use the prefixes from the [branching strategy](/docs/contributors/branching-strategy):
  `feature/`, `fix/`, `docs/`, `chore/`.
</Accordion>

## Reviews

<Accordion title="Why did my PR get so many comments?">
  Reviewers comment to make the change and the codebase better. The
  standards are documented in [code reviews](/docs/contributors/code-reviews);
  the comments are the same standards applied to your work.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I disagree with a review comment?">
  Yes, with evidence. Explain your reasoning. The review is a discussion,
  not a verdict, though maintainers have final authority.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I get my PR approved?">
  Meet the [pull request checklist](/docs/contributors/pull-requests), respond
  to comments, and keep the change small and focused.
</Accordion>

## The community

<Accordion title="I am nervous about asking questions. Is that okay?">
  It is completely normal, and the answer is always to ask anyway. The
  [community guidelines](/docs/contributors/community-guidelines) describe a
  community built to welcome questions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How are contributors recognized?">
  Through the [recognition](/docs/contributors/recognition) program: the
  contributors page, release notes, highlights and special recognition.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I be paid for contributing?">
  Some high impact work is rewarded through the DAO and grants. Most work is
  volunteered. See [Why Contribute](/docs/contributors/why-contribute) and
  [Governance](/docs/ecosystem/governance).
</Accordion>

## Security and standards

<Accordion title="I found a vulnerability. What do I do?">
  Report it privately through the [responsible disclosure](/docs/contributors/responsible-disclosure)
  process. Do not file it as a public issue.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I want to change a standard. How?">
  Standards change through the [specification lifecycle](/docs/standards/specification-lifecycle).
  Start a discussion and follow the process from there.
</Accordion>

<Callout type="info" title="Did not find your answer?">
  The [troubleshooting](/docs/contributors/troubleshooting) page handles
  problems, and the [community guidelines](/docs/contributors/community-guidelines)
  list the channels where the community answers questions every day.
</Callout>
