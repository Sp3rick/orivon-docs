# DAO Plan & Rewards

Here we explain the decentralization and DAO plan for contributors, devs and investors at *Orivon Foundation*

As a community driven project with the goal to create something willing to change Web3 forever, Orivon has to be based on a strong foundation, a Decentralized Public Company that can last for a long time and become a referring point for the Web3 space

Orivon has the potential to become one of the most important DAO's and companies in the world, so we need a system to keep all of us joint together keeping Orivon solid and prospering in a long term view

That's the reason we will eventually launch *The Orivon DAO*


## DAO Plan

Remember, *Orivon Foundation* is a community driven project, that's why, even while the On-Chain DAO isn't yet launched, contributors like you will be able to exercise control on the project

We split that decentralization process on 2 parts:  
- ***Pre-DAO*** phase *(current)*
- ***After-DAO*** phase *(On-Chain DAO with treasury)*

The switch between these 2 phases begins once the last big funding to build the Orivon Browser comes, it's also the moment where the first earnings begins entering the project, which are going to be sent directly to the DAO Treasury

Regarding the After-DAO phase there isn't that much to talk about, simply, *Orivon Foundation* management will be trough it  
<small>As the project matures we are exploring the organization plan about how the on-chain DAO is going to work</small>

But regarding the Pre-DAO phase we are going to explain a bit more

### Pre-DAO phase

Us of Orivon Foundation, we do our best for the success of Orivon and its community

As the project and community matures, more control power over *Orivon Foundation* will be gradually passed to the Contributors

That's accomplished within our [Discord](https://discord.gg/DuRg87MvgD) server, where we will gradually move from simple community polls for decision making, to eventually, allow contributors to make by themselves proposals and polls and act on behalf of the *Orivon Foundation*

By this way, the *Orivon Foundation* will already behave as a DAO before its actually launched on-chain and everyone will be able to get it's piece of ownership on the project as soon as you gain enough consensus

### After-DAO phase

After the Orivon DAO is launched all of the decisions to manage the official Orivon resources (such as Domains, Software, Treasury etc..) will be managed exclusively trough that

By its foundation it will be like mosts DAO's, with voting power related to the amount of tokens hold and decentralized-enough power  
Additional rules for specific Orivon needs may be developed, chosen along with the Core Team, Investors and community

The main DAO goal is to keep innovating on Web3 field, potentially making the Orivon DAO the most important on Web3 Industry  
For the rest, it will receive all of its earning into its Treasury, for more about this check [**Economic Strategy**](/docs/economical-strategy)

### Monetary payments

<img src={MonetaryDistribution} alt="MonetaryDistribution" width="100"/>

As already cited on many parts of this documentation, by its own nature, Orivon requires strong approval from Web3 to get success  
Meaning that strong community and investment supports is a great signal to indicate that it is probably going to work

If we are not able to move a mass of contributors and investments to make Orivon true in an organic way, we can't expect to be able to bring Orivon to the Web3, and then worldwide masses once it is done  
For that reason organic growth is the pre-requisite for us to proceed with Orivon success

That's the reason **we aren't available to boost our growth trough any kind of paid marketing on that initial phase**  
Since it does not align with our goal  

We estimate monetary payment of contributions **only after investors actually comes to back us** on that  
And that won't happen until we already had a strong community that has only been paid with *Tokens*, truly believing the idea

**Our current after-funding Monetary Payment plan** is currently pointed around *Technical Development*, even tough we aim to include some targeted campaigns to support some other roles at the project

As example, some Growth-side roles support or small grants to Core Team members

*Obviously that does change even more after the **Build Funding** is completed, with launched DAO and Orivon Browser out, Orivon will be an actual Web3 company where all of its workers are going to be regularly paid*

### Developers plan

<img src={DevelopersPlan} alt="DevelopersPlan" width="350"/>

For developers we share better insights, since devs are a core part for the Orivon success

As you have read from the [Roadmap](/docs/roadmap), our plan is to go for funding once we have a stable development team

To reward your efforts we share the Pre-Funding and After-Funding phase recompense plan  
Hopefully with that section you can orientate better on how things are going to be

#### PRE-FUNDING PHASE

Before funding comes, developer efforts are going to be paid at 100% by DAO Tokens  
For that reason all of you work and contribution will be kept and tracked at [**Orivon Browser Github**](https://github.com/OrivonBrowser/)

Developer contributions on that phase often won't be just hand-on code writing, but also [improving the Technical Word-written design or Implementation](https://orivonbrowser.github.io/internal-docs/docs/developer-work/#project-design-contribution), showing commitment/availability to the project and then MVP's / prototypes of Orivon parts

Basically, on that phase, your developer work is much about demonstrating to Web3 and Investors that we have Technical Foundations, especially with many great developers ready to work at it full-time if paid

As your developer effort on the Pre-Funding phase is so essential, it will be greatly valued on the DAO portion you will receive

#### AFTER-FUNDING PHASE

Once funding arrives, Orivon will open doors to hiring

If you've been active in the Pre-Funding phase you will have higher priority to be evaluated for hiring at Orivon

#### SALARY SPLIT

To show both the commitment of devs on the *Orivon Foundation* itself and less pressure on investors, the salary is going to be splitted by cash and DAO tokens

The split is going to be a 60-80% cash and 40-20% tokens, it will be decided based on needs

Employed devs will be free choose to invest even more into Orivon by themselves and have less cash for more tokens instead
