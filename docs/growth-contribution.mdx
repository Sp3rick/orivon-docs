## Join the Core Team

<img src={EliteChat} alt="EliteChat" width="250" />
<small style={{ display: "block", marginBottom: "1rem" }}></small>

We already explained extensively how you can become part of Orivon Growth, since its the core engine for project success  
But a second important engine is the Core Team

Core Team is composed by the most important Committed, Trusted, and Coordinated contributors on the project

All of these 3 points are essentials to be part of the Core Team:

- **Commitment**: Work actively with the rest of the Team to bring Orivon forward
- **Trust**: Built with time, trust is required for official and management roles at Orivon, and this is so valuable to us
- **Coordinated**: Same basic mindset, timetables compatibility, are usually important things to compose a strong Core Team

The main step to get evaluated for the Core Team is signing up this form: [**Contributor Team Availability Form**](https://forms.gle/pz8cBxYn7P8CL3pM8)  
Open a Ticket as well to facilitate a faster reply [**# 🎟️open-ticket🎟️**](https://discord.gg/mrwsRcwFKk) 

## Partnership

<img src={Partnership} alt="Partnership" width="250" />
<small style={{ display: "block", marginBottom: "1rem" }}></small>

Partnerships are an alternative way to 'join' the Core Team  
It doesn't require the *committed,trusted,coordinated* triage that joining the Core Team requires

But still it gives you privileged direct access to the Core Team discussions

Partnerships are more about long term results  
These can be tracked with suited systems in addition to the self-reports and by ourselves directly

You are usually eligible for a Partnership with Orivon when you have particulars *'Elite Roles'*

Some examples:

- You are a KOL
- Fundraiser
- Investor
- Specific roles we were looking for (example: at [**#♟️│roles-openings**](https://discord.gg/Jf2XqjzM44))
- Founder/Co-Founder/Partnership Manager of another project
- And waay more...

For that case just opt to open a Ticket at [**# 🎟️open-ticket🎟️**](https://discord.gg/mrwsRcwFKk)  
Tickets gives you instant access to the whole Core Team so we will get easily in touch there
