| **Category** | Whitepaper · **Status** Published |
| **Reading time** | 45 min |

**Abstract:** The Orivon platform is a web3 browser and application runtime
that returns control of the web to its users. This paper frames the problems
of the modern web, defines the platform's architectural pillars, and outlines
the ecosystem it establishes: an open browser, a sandboxed runtime, a module
system, user-controlled identity, and decentralized infrastructure.

**Abstract:** A browser is the user's front door to the internet, yet today's
browsers optimize for the interests of advertising platforms rather than
users. This paper describes the Orivon browser: its process model, tab
