Answers to common organizational and enterprise questions about Orivon. For
technical questions, see the [Developer FAQ](/docs/developers/faq) or
[Contributor FAQ](/docs/contributors/faq). For community questions, see the
[Community FAQ](/docs/community/faq).

      q: "What is Orivon?",
      a: "Orivon is an open-source browser and platform that returns control of the web to its users. It is built on WebAssembly, capability-based security, and open standards. The project is governed by its community under the Apache License 2.0."

      q: "Who owns Orivon?",
      a: "The community. Orivon is governed by contributors through an Apache-style meritocratic model: maintainers own repositories, working groups coordinate topics, and a Technical Steering Committee sets technical direction. No single company or individual controls the project. The Orivon Foundation (planned 2027) will hold trademarks and manage funds as a legal steward."

      q: "Is Orivon a cryptocurrency or blockchain project?",
