This Privacy Policy describes how the Orivon project ("we," "us," "our") handles
personal data when you use the Orivon browser, visit our websites, participate
in our community, or interact with our services.

  yet undergone formal legal review. Placeholders marked **[LEGAL REVIEW]**
  indicate sections requiring attorney validation before this policy is
  considered final for regulatory compliance (GDPR, CCPA, etc.).
</Callout>

## Data we collect

### 1. Orivon Browser (desktop application)
**By default: Zero telemetry. No unique identifiers. No network requests except:**
- Explicit user actions (navigating, downloading, updating).
- Certificate validation (standard TLS).
- Extension/store requests initiated by user.

**Opt-in data (only if user enables in Settings):**
| Data | Purpose | Retention | Shared |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crash reports (minidump) | Stability debugging | 90 days | Aggregated only |
| Performance traces (local) | Performance optimization | 30 days | Never |
| Feature usage counters | Product prioritization | 180 days | Aggregated, anonymized |

<Callout type="info" title="Opt-in is explicit">
  No data leaves your device without your informed consent. Settings default
  to "Off." You can view exactly what would be sent before enabling.
</Callout>

## Data sharing

We **do not sell personal data**. We share data only:

| Recipient | Purpose | Safeguards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hosting providers** (Vercel, GitHub Pages, etc.) | Serve websites | DPAs, EU Standard Contractual Clauses |
| **Email provider** (Buttondown, etc.) | Send newsletter | DPA, no secondary use |
| **Security researchers** | Coordinated disclosure | Minimal, case-specific, NDA where needed |
| **Legal authorities** | Valid legal process | Only what is legally required; notice where permitted |

We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers, or AI training companies.

## Children's privacy

Orivon is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in EU). We do not
knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have,
contact privacy@orivon.dev for immediate deletion.

## Security

| Measure | Implementation |
| --- | --- |
| **Encryption in transit** | TLS 1.2+ everywhere; HSTS; certificate pinning where feasible |
| **Encryption at rest** | Database encryption; disk encryption on servers |
| **Access control** | Least privilege; MFA for admin; audit logs |
| **Data minimization** | Collect only what is necessary; auto-delete per schedule |
| **Incident response** | 72-hour breach notification (GDPR); see [Security Policy](/docs/project/security-policy) |
