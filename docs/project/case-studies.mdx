Case studies are deep dives into real Orivon deployments: the problem, the
solution, the architecture, the results, and the lessons learned. This page
documents the **template and process** for future case studies.

## Case study template

Every case study follows this structure:

### 1. Executive Summary (150 words)
- Organization, industry, size.
- Challenge in one sentence.
- Solution in one sentence.
- Key result (metric).

### 2. Organization Profile
| Field | Example |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Name** | Acme Corporation |
| **Industry** | Financial Services |
| **Size** | 5,000 employees |
| **Region** | North America, Europe |
| **Orivon Use Since** | 2026-Q3 |

### 3. The Challenge
- Business/technical problem.
- Why existing solutions fell short.
- Requirements (security, compliance, scale, customization).

### 4. Why Orivon
- Specific capabilities that addressed the challenge.
- Comparison with alternatives considered.
- Decision factors (open source, capability model, standards, etc.).

### 5. Architecture & Implementation
- **Deployment model** (Self-hosted, Registry-Only, Hybrid).
- **Architecture diagram** (component layout, data flows).
- **Key components used** (Runtime, Browser, Registry, Identity, Modules).
- **Custom modules developed** (purpose, capabilities).
- **Integration points** (IdP, SIEM, CI/CD, monitoring).
- **Timeline** (POC → Pilot → Production).

### 6. Configuration & Policy
- **Capability policies** (allowlists, denylists).
- **Module allowlists** (verified, internal, partner).
- **Identity integration** (DID method, IdP, VC flows).
- **Network/egress controls**.
- **Observability setup** (metrics, logs, traces, alerts).

### 7. Results & Metrics
| Metric | Before | After | Improvement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Security incidents** | 12/yr | 0 | 100% reduction |
| **Module deploy time** | 2 weeks | 2 hours | 98% faster |
| **Developer onboarding** | 5 days | 4 hours | 90% faster |
| **Compliance audit prep** | 3 months | 3 weeks | 75% faster |
| **Infrastructure cost** | $X | $Y | Z% change |

### 8. Lessons Learned
- What worked well.
- What was harder than expected.
- Surprises (good and bad).
- What they'd do differently.

### 9. Advice for Others
- Top 3 recommendations for similar organizations.
- Pitfalls to avoid.
- Resources that helped.

### 10. Future Plans
- Next phase of Orivon adoption.
- Upcoming modules, integrations, expansions.

## Submission process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Express interest">
    Email case-studies@orivon.dev or mention in commercial support channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft">
    Orivon team provides template; customer fills Sections 1–3, 5–6.
    Orivon helps with Sections 4, 7–10 (technical accuracy).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Customer legal/comms review; Orivon technical review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Mutual sign-off; schedule publication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Published on this site, in newsletter, shared on social.
    Customer may republish on their channels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Anonymization options

Organizations may choose:
- **Full attribution** — Name, logo, quotes, metrics.
- **Anonymous** — "A Fortune 500 financial services firm," aggregated metrics.
- **Hybrid** — Industry/region/size public; name/logo private.
