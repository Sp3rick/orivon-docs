Success stories are the human side of the Orivon ecosystem — shorter, more
narrative, and focused on the people and teams behind the work. They
complement the technical depth of [Case Studies](/docs/project/case-studies).

## Story types

| Type | Focus | Length | Audience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Contributor Journey** | From first PR to maintainer/lead | 500–800 words | Contributors, newcomers |
| **Project Launch** | Shipping an ecosystem project | 600–1000 words | Builders, evaluators |
| **Community Impact** | Meetup, mentorship, hackathon outcomes | 400–700 words | Community organizers |
| **Organizational Adoption** | Team/org adopting Orivon (lighter than case study) | 800–1200 words | Decision-makers, peers |
| **Research Breakthrough** | Paper, reproduction, novel result | 500–800 words | Researchers, engineers |

## Story template

### Header
- **Title** — Compelling, specific ("How Team X Reduced Module Deploy from Weeks to Hours").
- **Type** — One of the above.
- **Author(s)** — Name, role, organization (or "Orivon Community").
- **Date** — Publication date.
- **Tags** — `contributor-journey`, `project-launch`, `community-impact`, `org-adoption`, `research`.

### Body Structure

#### 1. The Hook (1–2 paragraphs)
- Who, what, why it matters.
- The "before" state — pain point or opportunity.

#### 2. The Journey (3–5 paragraphs)
- **Discovery** — How they found Orivon.
- **First steps** — First PR, first module, first meetup.
- **Challenges** — What was hard; how they overcame.
- **Community** — Who helped (mentors, WGs, Discord, forums).
- **Breakthrough** — The moment it clicked / shipped.

#### 3. The Outcome (1–2 paragraphs)
- Concrete results (metrics, launch, promotion, grant, recognition).
- Impact on them, their team, their users, the ecosystem.

#### 4. Reflection (1 paragraph)
- What they'd tell their past self.
- Advice for others starting the same path.
- What's next for them.

#### 5. Resources (bullet list)
- Links to PRs, modules, docs, talks, repos, discussions.

## Story guidelines

| Do | Don't |
| :--- | :--- |
| Be specific — "reduced 3 weeks to 2 hours" | Be vague — "made things faster" |
| Name the people who helped | Take solo credit for team work |
| Share the struggles | Polish away all friction |
| Link to real artifacts (PRs, repos) | Reference private/internal-only things |
| Write in your voice | Sound like a press release |

## Success story vs. Case study

| Aspect | Success Story | Case Study |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Length** | 500–1200 words | 1500–3000 words |
| **Tone** | Personal, narrative | Technical, analytical |
| **Focus** | People, journey, impact | Architecture, metrics, decisions |
| **Author** | Protagonist | Orivon + Customer |
| **Review** | Light edit | Legal + Technical + Comms |
| **Audience** | Community, builders, peers | Evaluators, architects, execs |
