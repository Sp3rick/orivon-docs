The Orivon project plans to establish an **Advisory Board** to provide
strategic guidance on technical direction, governance, ecosystem growth, and
long-term sustainability.

<Callout type="info" title="Coming Soon">
  This page is reserved for future advisory board members. No advisors have
  been appointed as of July 2026. The advisory board structure will be defined
  as part of the [Orivon Foundation](/docs/ecosystem/governance#the-orivon-foundation)
  incorporation planned for 2027.
</Callout>

## Planned advisory board structure

### Purpose
Provide independent, expert counsel to the [Technical Steering Committee](/docs/community/technical-steering-committee)
and [Orivon Foundation](/docs/ecosystem/governance#the-orivon-foundation) on:

- Long-term technical strategy and research priorities.
- Governance evolution and community health.
- Ecosystem development and partnership strategy.
- Standards engagement and policy advocacy.
- Funding diversification and sustainability.
- Legal, regulatory, and compliance landscape.

### Composition (Planned)
| Seat Type | Count | Selection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Technical advisors** | 3–5 | Recognized experts in WebAssembly, browser architecture, security, distributed systems |
| **Governance advisors** | 2–3 | Open-source governance practitioners (Apache, Linux Foundation, CNCF experience) |
| **Ecosystem advisors** | 2–3 | Builders of successful open ecosystems, platform strategists |
| **Policy/legal advisors** | 1–2 | Technology policy, open-source licensing, nonprofit law |
| **Community advisors** | 2–3 | Community builders, developer advocates, regional leaders |

### Selection process (Planned)
1. **Nomination** — By TSC, Foundation board, or community.
2. **Vetting** — Conflict of interest review, expertise verification.
3. **Approval** — TSC consensus + Foundation board ratification.
4. **Term** — 2 years, renewable once; staggered for continuity.
5. **Compensation** — Modest honorarium or grant to advisor's institution; no equity.

### Advisor responsibilities (Planned)
- Attend quarterly advisory board meetings (virtual).
- Review and comment on strategic documents (roadmap, governance proposals).
- Provide ad-hoc counsel on request (reasonable availability).
- Make introductions to relevant networks (standards bodies, funders, talent).
- Publicly associated with the project (name, affiliation, role listed).

## Related governance bodies

| Body | Role | Relationship |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Technical Steering Committee** | Technical authority | Receives advisory input; makes decisions |
| **Orivon Foundation** | Legal/financial steward | Ratifies appointments; funds honoraria |
| **Working Groups** | Topic coordination | May request advisor input on specific topics |
| **Community** | All participants | Benefits from advisor-guided strategy |

<Callout type="success" title="Advisors advise; community decides">
  The advisory board provides counsel. Decision-making authority remains with
  the TSC (technical) and Foundation (legal/financial), both accountable to
  the community. Advisors do not vote on project decisions.
</Callout>
