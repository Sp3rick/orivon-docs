## Response Expectations

| Channel | Acknowledgment | Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Security Email** | 48 hours | Per severity (see [Security Policy](/docs/project/security-policy)) |
| **Press Email** | 1 business day | Varies by request |
| **Enterprise Email** | 1 business day | Discovery call within 5 days |
| **Partnerships Email** | 2 business days | Discovery call within 10 days |
| **General Email** | 1–2 business days | Varies |
| **GitHub Issue** | Community-dependent | Per priority |
| **Discord/Forum** | Minutes–Hours | Community-dependent |

## Escalation

If you don't receive a response within the expected timeframe:

1. **Follow up** — Reply to your original email.
2. **Try alternate channel** — e.g., if email is slow, try Discord `#urgent`.
3. **Escalate** — Mention "escalation" in subject; reaches project leads.
