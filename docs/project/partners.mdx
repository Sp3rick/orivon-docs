The Orivon partner ecosystem includes organizations that build on, integrate
with, extend, or distribute the Orivon platform. This directory will grow as
formal partnerships are established.

<Callout type="info" title="Partner directory seeding">
  The partner program is being designed for 2027 launch. This page contains
  placeholders for future partners. Organizations interested in partnership
  should contact partnerships@orivon.dev.
</Callout>

## Partner categories

| Category | Description | Benefits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Technology Partners** | Integrate Orivon into their stack; build on Orivon APIs | Early access, co-marketing, roadmap input, technical liaison |
| **Integration Partners** | Connect Orivon to external protocols, chains, standards | Standards collaboration, joint testing, shared roadmap |
| **Distribution Partners** | Help users discover and adopt Orivon | Revenue share (planned), co-branded resources, marketplace listing |
| **Ecosystem Partners** | Build applications, modules, services for Orivon users | Grants priority, showcase, marketplace, community access |
| **Research Partners** | Collaborate on research, standards, open problems | Research access, joint publications, grant collaboration |
| **Professional Services Partners** | Deliver consulting, implementation, training | Certification, lead referral, co-delivery |

## Becoming a partner

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify fit">
    Review categories and determine where your organization aligns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit interest">
    Fill out the partner interest form (To Be Announced) or email partnerships@orivon.dev.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discovery call">
    Partnerships team reviews and schedules a technical/business alignment call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agreement">
    Sign partner agreement (non-exclusive, no governance rights, public listing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Onboarding">
    Technical integration, directory listing, joint announcement, partner portal access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Partnership principles

| Principle | Practice |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Non-exclusive** | Partners free to work with other platforms. |
| **No governance rights** | Partnership ≠ decision-making authority. |
| **Public by default** | Partnerships announced and listed publicly. |
| **Mutual value** | Both sides must benefit measurably. |
| **Standards-first** | Integrations follow published standards, not private APIs. |
| **Open collaboration** | Work in public where possible (repos, forums, specs). |

## Partner responsibilities

- Maintain integration to current Orivon standards.
- Communicate breaking changes in advance (90 days preferred).
- Participate in security disclosure coordination.
- Keep directory listing accurate.
- Display partnership per [Brand Guidelines](/docs/project/brand-guidelines).
