Orivon's compliance roadmap documents current and planned certifications,
regulatory alignments, and industry standard adherence. This page is for
enterprise evaluators, auditors, and compliance officers.

<Callout type="warning" title="Certifications not yet obtained">
  **Orivon has not yet achieved any formal certifications.** All items marked
  "Certified" are **planned or in progress**. This page will be updated as
  certifications are achieved. Do not rely on this page for compliance evidence
  until certifications are explicitly confirmed with dates and scope.
</Callout>

## Compliance program structure

### Roles (Planned)
| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Compliance Officer** | Program ownership, auditor liaison |
| **Security Lead** | Technical controls, evidence collection |
| **Privacy Lead** | DPIA, DPA, data subject rights |
| **Legal Counsel** | Regulatory interpretation, contracts |

### Processes
| Process | Cadence | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Risk assessment** | Annual + trigger-based | Compliance Officer |
| **Control testing** | Quarterly | Security Lead |
| **Vendor assessment** | Annual | Compliance Officer |
| **Incidence response test** | Semi-annual | Security Lead |
| **Policy review** | Annual | Legal Counsel |
| **Training** | Annual + onboarding | All roles |

### Evidence management
- **Centralized repository:** `orivon-compliance` (private, access-controlled).
- **Artifact types:** Policies, procedures, test results, audit reports, attestations.
- **Retention:** Per regulatory requirement (minimum 7 years for SOC/ISO).
- **Access:** Need-to-know; auditors granted time-limited access.

## Compliance for enterprise evaluators

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| **Can I use Orivon in regulated environments?** | Platform architecture supports compliance (local-first, no telemetry, signed releases, SBOMs). Formal certifications in progress. |

| **What is your vulnerability disclosure policy?** | See [Security Policy](/docs/project/security-policy) and [Responsible Disclosure](/docs/contributors/responsible-disclosure). |
